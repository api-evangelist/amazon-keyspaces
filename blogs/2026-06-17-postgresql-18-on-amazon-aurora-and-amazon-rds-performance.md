Part 1 of a two-part series examining key PostgreSQL 18 features on Amazon Aurora and Amazon RDS, focusing on query optimization improvements. It covers skip scan optimization for multicolumn indexes, enhanced EXPLAIN output, automatic removal of unnecessary self-joins, and several vacuum and autovacuum improvements.
